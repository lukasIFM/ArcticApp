Privacy Policy

ArcticApp · International Fabric Machines · Effective 9 October 2026

ArcticApp does not collect any data. It has no user account, no network upload path, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. Everything the app records stays on your device unless you choose to export and send it yourself.

What the app records, and where it goes

During a session ArcticApp stores the following in its own private storage on your device:

  • Sensor readings from connected IFM garments — temperature, heat flux, optically derived heart rate and perfusion, motion, and each unit's battery and storage state.
  • A participant identifier and mission label, if you enter them. These are free text and you choose what they contain.
  • Timestamped event marks you create, and any spoken note you attach to one.
  • Diagnostic logs describing the Bluetooth connection, used for troubleshooting.

None of this is transmitted to International Fabric Machines or to anyone else. We never receive it and have no way to access it.

Microphone

The microphone is used only to record a spoken note, and only while you are holding the MARK EVENT button. The app has no wake word and does not listen at any other time. Recordings are saved as audio files in the app's own folder on your device. They are never uploaded.

Bluetooth

Bluetooth is used solely to connect to IFM instrumented garments and, if the team feature is in use, to exchange wearer status with other handsets running ArcticApp within radio range. That exchange carries status information between devices directly and does not pass through any server. Bluetooth is not used for advertising, tracking or location.

Exporting your data

You can export a session as compressed CSV files. Exports are written to the app's own documents folder, reachable through the Files app. From there, anything you do with them — sending, copying, deleting — is entirely under your control and outside this app.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. There is no copy anywhere else, so there is nothing for you to request the deletion of and no account to close.

Children

ArcticApp is a professional instrument for trained personnel and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
