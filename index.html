ArcticApp

Support & documentation · International Fabric Machines

ArcticApp is a field data-collection instrument for cold-weather exercises. It reads from instrumented garments — the IFM fibre sensor range — over Bluetooth Low Energy, and records what they report for later analysis.

ArcticApp requires IFM instrumented garments. It has no simulated data mode. Without a garment connected it will launch, scan, find no devices and show no readings. That is correct behaviour, not a fault.

Getting started

  • Charge the garment and switch it on.
  • Open ArcticApp and allow Bluetooth when prompted.
  • Go to Settings → Assign garments, pick a slot, and choose your unit from the list of devices in range.
  • For a head unit, tap the matching cap identity so the app loads that unit's fitted calibration coefficients.
  • Put the garment on. Readings appear once the app confirms it is being worn.

Common questions

Nothing appears in the device list. Check that Bluetooth is on and that ArcticApp has Bluetooth permission in iOS Settings. A garment already connected to another phone will not advertise and cannot be seen — disconnect it there first.

A garment is connected but shows “Not worn”. The app will not report a temperature from a garment that is not on a body, because an air-temperature reading presented as a person's temperature is worse than no reading. It will start reporting shortly after the garment is worn.

Spoken notes say the microphone is unavailable. Allow microphone access in iOS Settings → ArcticApp. The microphone is live only while the MARK EVENT button is held; there is no wake word and the app does not listen between notes.

Where is my data? On your device. Sessions export as gzipped CSV into the app's own folder, which you can reach through the Files app under On My iPhone → ArcticApp.

Important

ArcticApp is a research and training instrument. It is not a medical device, it makes no diagnostic claim, and it must not be used to diagnose or treat anyone.

Core temperature is an estimate produced by a model, not a direct measurement. Alert thresholds are provisional study values and are not clinical criteria. Always follow your own cold-weather safety procedures.

Privacy

ArcticApp collects no data. Everything it records stays on your device. See the privacy policy.
